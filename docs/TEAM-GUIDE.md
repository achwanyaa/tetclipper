# tetclipper: how the team works

This guide is for everyone who uses tetclipper online: the editors who make the
videos and the communication team who check and post them.

## Who does what

- **Editors** make and edit clips, render them, and send them for review.
- **The communication team** watches each video, says whether it's good or
  needs work, and downloads good videos to post.
- **The admin** can do both, and chooses the AI model.

If you see the wrong screens, ask the admin to check your role.

## Editors

1. Open your clip. The tools are on the left; the video is in the middle; the
   timeline is along the bottom.
2. Edit as usual. To ask the AI for a change, use the **AI** tab and type what
   you want, for example "remove the dead air".
3. To put a logo or GIF on the video, open **Overlays** and press **Add a photo,
   GIF or video**. Drag it on the video to move it, pull a corner to make it
   bigger or smaller, and drag its bar on the timeline to choose when it shows.
4. Press **Render**. When it's done, press **Send for review**. You can add a
   short message for the team.
5. If the team sends it back, the clip opens on the **Review** tab with their
   notes. Click **jump there** to see the spot, fix it, and tick **I fixed
   this**. Then press **Render and send for review again**.

Handy keys: **Space** plays and pauses. **Delete** removes the overlay or effect
you have selected. **Undo** (top bar) takes back the last change.

## Communication team

1. Open **Review** in the top bar. The number next to it is how many videos are
   waiting.
2. Click a video to watch it.
3. If it's ready, press **Good**. It moves to the **Good** tab.
4. If something needs fixing, pause the video where the problem is, write a
   note, and press **Add note at …**. For something about the whole video, press
   **Note about the whole video**. Then press **Needs work**. The editor gets your
   notes.
5. To post a good video, press **Download video** and post it from your phone or
   computer as usual.

**Record** shows every video: who edited it, who reviewed it, how many times it
was sent back, and who downloaded it.

## Good to know

- A video sent back keeps all its notes, so you can see what changed between
  versions.
- If an editor sends a new version while you're watching an old one, you'll be
  asked to watch the new one first.
- Only the newest version of each video is kept for download.
